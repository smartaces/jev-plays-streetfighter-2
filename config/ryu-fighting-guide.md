# Ryu’s fighting guide — v13

Genesis SFII, Champion rules. Choose an attack that can reach, defend a threat, or move to set up an attack. Forward movement alone does not damage the opponent. Coaching is advice; current facts take priority.

- Compare options using `engagement.range_band`: **close (0–35)** quick normals or a touching throw; **poke (36–65)** medium punch, crouching kick or sweep against grounded legs; **entry (66–140)** hurricane kick, forward jump attack or fireball; **far (141+)** fireball or repositioning. These are rough reference-point distances, not exact hitboxes, minimum ranges or permissions. Moves overlap bands. The older `spacing` label is coarser.
- Check motion and `distance_after_reply_estimate`: a retreating target may leave punch range before inputs arrive. The forecast assumes unchanged velocity and is uncertain. Do not walk until touching when a poke or advancing attack can already threaten.
- Defend attacks that can reach. Ground attacks beyond the close band still matter. Intercept an early approaching jump with dragon punch; use grounded standing guard (retreat) for a late jump attack. Crouching block handles lows and ground projectiles. Ryu cannot block airborne.
- Ready and unthreatened: compare attacks. Forward jump kick attacks from above; reassess on landing for low kick or kick–fireball. Hurricane kick covers ground while attacking. Both can be countered; walking is not always safer. Attack an exposed landing or recovery without assuming a guaranteed combo.
- Fireballs are useful at range, including against Guile. `fireball_pace=available` permits them; `ease_off` asks for another useful tactic. Do not merely wait for it to reset. Repeated walking without attacking calls for a reachable attack or necessary defence.
- Healthy Ryu can take an attacking opportunity. Low health favours brief pokes, safe shots and counters over chasing. Protect a lead with spacing; seek damage when time is short and behind. Avoid permanent retreat or movement loops.

Readiness and reach are estimates. Reactions do not prove dizzy. Capability does not prove an active move. Recent damage does not identify which attack connected. Jev selects the action; the controller executes its buttons.
